### Introduction

In this stage, the focus of the work gradually shifts from feature development to reflection and analysis of existing design decisions, while also improving editing quality and strengthening logical consistency across the system.
This shift reflects a transition from implementation-focused work to a more critical evaluation of how design decisions influence usability, interaction flow, and overall system coherence.
Rather than focusing solely on building features, this stage also considers how different components of the system connect and whether the overall structure remains clear, consistent, and intuitive.
At this stage, evaluation remains conceptual rather than empirical, focusing on identifying what aspects of usability, interaction design, and system structure should be further examined in future testing stages.
This approach helps ensure that future evaluation is grounded in clear design priorities, including usability, accessibility, and user interaction clarity within the anonymous communication system.

---
### Prototype Review

The current prototype includes key interaction features such as room selection, chat navigation, floating action buttons, and card-based room display.
While the system is functional at a basic level, several usability considerations have emerged during development.
These observations suggest that interface structure and interaction clarity need to be carefully balanced, especially in anonymous communication systems where users rely heavily on visual and navigational cues.

---
### Evaluation Considerations

At this stage, evaluation remains conceptual rather than empirical, focusing on identifying what aspects of usability, interaction design, and system structure should be further examined in future stages.
The following areas have been identified as priorities for future evaluation:

---
### Usability Testing

Future evaluation should focus on whether users can complete core tasks such as joining a room, creating a room, and sending messages without guidance.
This will help determine whether the interaction flow is intuitive.

---
### Navigation Flow Evaluation

The navigation structure should be evaluated in terms of clarity and cognitive load.
In particular, it is important to assess whether users can easily understand how to move between random rooms, created rooms, and chat interfaces.

---
### Accessibility Considerations

The interface should be reviewed for readability, contrast, and responsiveness across different screen sizes.
This is particularly important due to the use of soft visual effects such as blur and transparency.

---
### Behavioural Interaction (Future Observation)

The anonymous nature of the system introduces uncertainty in user behaviour during interaction.
Future evaluation should consider whether anonymity reduces social pressure and increases engagement.

---
### Reflection

This week helped shift the focus from building features to understanding how the system should be evaluated.
Instead of adding new functionality, the prototype was analysed in terms of usability, interaction clarity, and potential limitations.
A key insight from this stage is that evaluation should be considered as part of the design process rather than an afterthought.
Future work will focus on refining the prototype based on these evaluation priorities.
